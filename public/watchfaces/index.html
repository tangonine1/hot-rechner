2. Overview

3. Data on your watch

To display information, the watch faces use only values provided by your watch's operating system: time, date, time zone with UTC offset and daylight saving time, and battery level. These values are only displayed on the watch; they are not stored or transmitted.

4. Complications

Some watch faces offer complication slots that you fill yourself with data from other apps (e.g. sunrise and sunset, or the holdover time from the HOT Calculator). This data is supplied to the watch face by the app you choose, and the watch face only displays it. Processing by these apps is governed by their own privacy policies; for the HOT Calculator, see here.

6. Permissions

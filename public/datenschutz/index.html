4. Standort und Wetterdaten (AutoLoc, METAR)

Standort (AutoLoc): Wenn Sie es erlauben, bestimmt die App beim Öffnen (höchstens alle 10 Minuten, nicht während ein Timer läuft) Ihren ungefähren Standort – nicht die genaue Position –, um den nächstgelegenen Flughafen einzutragen. Die Position wird nur auf Ihrem Gerät mit der in der App enthaltenen Flughafenliste verglichen; sie wird nicht gespeichert und weder an den Anbieter noch an Dritte übertragen. Den Standort ermittelt das Betriebssystem Ihres Geräts; je nach Ihren Geräteeinstellungen nutzt es dafür eigene Ortungsdienste (z. B. von Google oder Apple), auf die die App keinen Einfluss hat. Die App fragt einmal nach der Erlaubnis; ohne sie bleibt die App voll nutzbar, Sie geben den Flughafen dann selbst ein. Sie können die Erlaubnis jederzeit in den Geräteeinstellungen entziehen oder die Suche über 📍 im Flughafenfeld selbst neu starten.

Wetterdaten (METAR): Die App ruft die aktuelle Wettermeldung direkt bei aviationweather.gov ab, einem Dienst des National Weather Service der USA (NOAA, U.S. Department of Commerce) – entweder wenn Sie „METAR laden“ antippen (für den eingegebenen ICAO-Code) oder bei AutoLoc automatisch in einer einzigen Abfrage für die bis zu acht nächstgelegenen Flughäfen im Umkreis von 15 km. Übertragen werden nur die ICAO-Codes, nicht Ihre Position; technisch bedingt außerdem Ihre IP-Adresse, der Zeitpunkt und Geräteangaben im Rahmen der Internetverbindung. Weil die abgefragten Flughäfen in Ihrer Nähe liegen, lässt sich aus einer AutoLoc-Abfrage die Gegend ableiten, in der Sie sich befinden. Der Anbieter der App erhält diese Daten nicht.

Zweck ist die Anzeige der von Ihnen angeforderten Wetterdaten bzw. des nächstgelegenen Flughafens; Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO (AutoLoc nutzen Sie, indem Sie die Standortfreigabe erteilen). Der Empfänger sitzt in den USA. Für diese US-Behörde besteht kein Angemessenheitsbeschluss der EU-Kommission; die Übermittlung erfolgt, weil sie zur Erfüllung Ihrer Anfrage erforderlich ist (Art. 49 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO). Es besteht das Risiko, dass US-Behörden auf die Daten zugreifen und Ihnen dagegen keine gleichwertigen Rechtsbehelfe zustehen. Ohne Abruf können Sie alle Werte von Hand eingeben.

Sie wird gelöscht, wenn Sie den Firmencode in der App entfernen, spätestens 60 Tage nach der letzten Nutzung. Der Anbieter erfährt nicht, wer einen Code nutzt. Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO.

2. Overview

The provider only receives the information you send yourself with a problem report (section 4). Personal data is processed only in the cases described below and only for the purposes stated there.

3. Data on your device

The app stores locally on your device: settings (language, temperature unit, aircraft category), recently used airports and de-icing fluids, the running timer and the de-icing log (times, airport, METAR, outside air temperature, holdover time). This data does not leave your device unless you export the log as a PDF yourself and share it via an app of your choice.

The purpose is to provide the app's functions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (use of the app at your request). The data is kept until you delete it in the app (delete log; the log holds at most the last 300 entries), clear the app data in the Android settings or uninstall the app.

4. Location and weather data (AutoLoc, METAR)

Location (AutoLoc): If you allow it, the app determines your approximate location – not your precise position – when it is opened (at most every 10 minutes, not while a timer is running) in order to enter the nearest airport. The position is only compared on your device with the airport list contained in the app; it is not stored and is not transmitted to the provider or to third parties. Your location is determined by your device's operating system; depending on your device settings it may use its own location services (e.g. from Google or Apple), over which the app has no control. The app asks for permission once; without it the app remains fully usable and you enter the airport yourself. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the device settings or restart the search yourself with 📍 in the airport field.

Weather data (METAR): The app requests the current weather report directly from aviationweather.gov, a service of the US National Weather Service (NOAA, U.S. Department of Commerce) – either when you tap "Load METAR" (for the entered ICAO code) or automatically with AutoLoc in a single request for up to eight nearest airports within 15 km. Only the ICAO codes are transmitted, not your position; for technical reasons also your IP address, the time and device information as part of the internet connection. Because the requested airports are near you, an AutoLoc request reveals the area you are in. The provider of the app does not receive this data.

The purpose is to display the weather data you requested or the nearest airport; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you use AutoLoc by granting the location permission). The recipient is located in the USA. There is no adequacy decision of the European Commission for this US authority; the transfer takes place because it is necessary to fulfil your request (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR). There is a risk that US authorities access the data without you having equivalent legal remedies. Without the request you can enter all values by hand.

Company version (company code): If you redeem a company code from your employer, the app loads the company settings (company name, validity, number of licences) once from hot.emb500.de and afterwards checks at most once a day whether they still apply. The file is encrypted and is requested only by an identifier derived from the code; the code itself, your name or your position are not transmitted. For technical reasons the hosting provider Cloudflare receives your IP address and the time. To count the licences, the app reports a randomly generated device number that is created only in the app and reveals nothing about you or your device; only this number and the time of the last check are stored. It is deleted when you remove the company code in the app, at the latest 60 days after last use. The provider does not learn who uses a code. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Report a problem: With “Report a problem” you can voluntarily report a problem. The following is then sent: the selected kind of problem and its effect, your description, where applicable the value in the paper table, the “safety occurrence” flag, if you wish the inputs and result of the last calculation, a screenshot of the app and a photo, and automatically the app version, table edition, app language, device class and operating system version (no device model, no device identifier), the date (without time), a random report number and, with a company licence, its identifier (not the code). The report is transmitted via hot-rechner.guido-marhenke.workers.dev (Cloudflare) to the provider, stored there for 180 days and e-mailed to the provider via the mailing service Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, 55 rue d’Amsterdam, 75008 Paris, France). Without a connection the report stays on the device for up to 30 days and is sent automatically. The purpose is to detect and fix errors in the app; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your voluntary report) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a correct, safety-relevant app). You can request deletion by e-mail stating the report number.

Table currency (all users) and automatic table update (Pro): When opened, at most every six hours, the app loads a small file from hot.emb500.de that states which edition of the FAA and Transport Canada tables is current. If that edition is newer than the tables in the app, the app shows a notice. With Pro, the app also loads the new tables, digitally signed by the provider, from hot.emb500.de and checks them on the device before use. Only the file is requested; no information about you, your device, your location or your subscription is transmitted. For technical reasons the hosting provider Cloudflare receives your IP address and the time; the provider keeps no access logs. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing current tables as a function of the app).
